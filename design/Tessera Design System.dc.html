Name and logo direction

Pick
Tessera

Latin for a single mosaic tile. Mark: a 2×2 grid of squared tiles; the fourth has morphed to a softer shape and takes the accent, the one tile the library has placed for you.

Still

A still frame, and a promise of calm. Mark: a rounded viewfinder with one off-center dot at the rule-of-thirds point. Strong, but generic and hard to own in search.

Northlight

The even, indirect light painters prefer. Mark: a quarter-circle of light entering a dark square. Warm and calm, but long at small sizes and less about organizing.

Tessera. The name describes the product's core job: every photo is a tile that the library sorts into a larger picture, which differentiates it from storage-first competitors. It is short, ownable, pronounceable across languages, and its mark is built from the same shapes as the UI's own grid and shape-morph selection state.
