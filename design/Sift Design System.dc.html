01

Name & logo direction

Chosen
Sift

Mark: a 3 × 3 grid where the last tiles are still settling, round and translucent, into place. The grid is the library; the motion is the organizing. Animates as the launcher splash and the "organizing" state.

Hearth

Mark: an arch, a doorway into a home. Speaks to "your photos stay with you." Warm, but leans domestic and says little about organization or actions.

Plate

Mark: a photographic plate with a folded corner, the frame turning into a document. Nods to tickets and receipts becoming actions. Reads as archival and slightly technical.

Why Sift: it names the core behavior, a library that sorts itself, as a short, calm verb that also works inside the product ("Sifted 214 photos on device"). Its mark is pure grid geometry, so it stays neutral next to any photo and doubles as the app's organizing animation. Trademark search pending.

Light weight keeps chrome recessive against photos. Two product-specific glyphs carry the trust story and are never substituted: memory = on device, cloud = left the device.
